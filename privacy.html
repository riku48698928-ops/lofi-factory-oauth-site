1. Overview

LoFi Factory Analytics ("the Application") is a personal-use, non-commercial application operated by its developer. It is used to review performance information for the developer's own YouTube channel and videos.

2. Information the Application accesses

When the developer authorizes the Application through Google OAuth, the Application may access information made available through these read-only scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly

This may include YouTube channel identifiers and channel information; video identifiers, titles, publication information, and related metadata; read-only YouTube Analytics metrics such as views, watch time, audience engagement, and traffic-source information where available; and OAuth credentials and tokens needed to maintain authorized access.

The Application does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or other unrelated Google services.

3. How information is used

Information is used solely to analyze the performance of the developer's own YouTube videos, generate internal reports, compare experiments, and improve the developer's automated content-production workflow.

Information obtained from Google APIs is not used for advertising, user profiling for third parties, or sale of personal data.

4. Storage and security

Application data and OAuth credentials are stored in a private, self-hosted environment controlled by the developer. Reasonable access controls are used to restrict access to those files and data.

This public website does not contain OAuth tokens, client secrets, analytics databases, or other private operational data.

5. Sharing and disclosure

The Application does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

Data may be processed by Google as necessary to provide the YouTube and Google OAuth APIs, subject to Google's own terms and policies.

6. Retention and deletion

Analytics records may be retained for historical comparison and internal analysis. OAuth credentials are retained only while the Application remains authorized and in use.

The developer may delete locally stored analytics records or OAuth credentials at any time. Revoking the Application's Google Account access prevents future authorized API access until authorization is granted again.

7. Revoking access

Google Account access can be reviewed or revoked through the Google Account security and third-party access settings. After revocation, the Application will no longer be able to access protected YouTube data unless it is authorized again.

8. Cookies and website tracking

This informational website does not use advertising, analytics services, tracking pixels, or application-specific cookies.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Application's data use or OAuth permissions change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

For questions about this Application or this Privacy Policy, contact the developer through the public GitHub profile: github.com/riku48698928-ops.
